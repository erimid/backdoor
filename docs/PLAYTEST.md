# Playtest log: Backdoor hacking prototype (v2)

The question: **is hacking fun enough that you want another round?**

v2 changes the shape of a job: sneak in, grab the data, then **BREACH**. ENTRY
burns, a different gold exit opens, the Sentries hunt you and the admin's trace
clock starts. You get paid in dollars (contract + caches − heat + spare turns), and
the three stars are: get out, beat PAR, and the job's bonus.

## How to run it

1. Play each job about 10 times. Start with Job 01. Your first run is always the
   tutorial night; tips pop up as you play, and **How to play** on the briefing has
   the full rules.
2. Mix **Tonight** and **New night** on the job board. Tonight is the same layout
   for everyone today. New night (unlocked after your first win on a job) rolls
   a different one: what the hidden nodes hold, where the Sentries start and which
   exit BREACH opens.
3. After every run, answer **"Do you want another round?"** under the results, and
   add a note if anything felt like a chore, confusing or great.
4. When you're done, open **Playtest log** on the job board, tap **Copy**, and
   paste it into the project thread (or below).

The game records the night, payout, stars, heat, turns, route, programs, AI deals
and how close a loss was, so you only need to write what it can't see.

## Routes to try

**Job 01 · Corner Store POS**

- Slip past the Sentry on the left (CAM-01 > SWITCH > SALES DB). Grab the SWITCH
  cache on the way if the timing works.
- Brute the firewall (TILL-2 > FIREWALL > POS-SRV): instant but loud. The Sentry
  comes to look.
- The side path past PRINTER and BACKUP: one is loot and one is a trap. Sniff
  first, or gamble.
- **BREACH exits:** the way out is WIFI or TILL-2. Try a run where you Sniff early
  to learn which, and one where you only find out on Extract.
- **Greed:** caches pay double after Extract. Is the detour worth the heat?

**Job 02 · City Bank, Branch 7**

- Trust UNKNOWN and go through it: the shortcut costs 15% of the payout.
- Middle: Brute the firewall from WIFI.
- Left: MAIL-02 has the password for AUTH-01. Decoy and Worm handle Sentry B
  (the Worm stun is the bonus star). ADMIN-PC has a cache one hop off the vault.
- Right: past PRINT-04, BACKUP, LEDGER and HR-DB. One is a trap, one is cash and
  one is a spare Brute.
- **BREACH exits:** PRINT-04 or MAIL-02. On Extract, UNKNOWN offers to freeze the
  Sentries for 25% (on top of the shortcut's cut if you took it).

**Tonight vs New night**

- Replay Tonight after a loss (RETRY plays the same night in one tap). Does
  knowing the layout make the retry feel fair?
- Then try New night a few times. Do different nights feel different, or the same
  board with a shuffle?

## Things to watch for

- **Waiting turns.** Holding (ending a turn with actions left) now banks a third
  action. Does holding feel like a choice or a chore?
- **The hunt.** After Extract the Sentries come straight at you. Being caught should
  feel like your mistake, not a trap. Note any night where it felt unwinnable.
- **Trace clock.** +1 heat every turn after BREACH. Too fast, too slow?
- **Greed vs PAR.** Are you chasing caches, or racing for the exit?
- **UNKNOWN's deals.** Refusing is free; trusting costs 15% (shortcut) or 25% (freeze). Is either one an
  obvious pick every time?
- **The results screen.** Payout count-up, stars, NEW BEST: does it pull you into
  another round, or do you skip past it?
